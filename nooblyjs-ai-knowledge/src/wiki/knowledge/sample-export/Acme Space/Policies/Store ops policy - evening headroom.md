# Store ops policy · evening headroom

> Sample data for the NooblyJS "PRD-031 Same-day delivery slots" story. Not a real Acme Retail document.

Type: Policy · Owner: Store operations · Reviewed: 2025-08-04

Stores may add up to 20% manual headroom to slot capacity for evening delivery windows (17:00 to 21:00), when the store manager expects more pickers than the roster shows. Headroom is added in the store console and lasts for that evening only.

## Rules

- Headroom above 20% needs the regional operations manager.
- Headroom never lifts a slot above the van cap: slot capacity is still limited by vans in the window.
- A store that adds headroom and then misses more than 5% of its evening deliveries loses the option for 30 days.

This policy was written before same-day delivery slots. It has not been reviewed against the new slot model.
