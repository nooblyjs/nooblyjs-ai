## Problem

Customers call to ask where their driver is. Show the driver on a map from dispatch to the door. Sample data.

## Goals

| Measure | Today | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calls asking where the driver is, per week | 1,900 | 600 |
| Out-for-delivery orders whose tracking is opened | 0% | 70% |
| Age of the driver's position on the map, p95 | none | 30 s |

## User stories

- As a customer, I can see my driver on a map from dispatch to my door so I know my order is on its way.
- As a customer, I can see when my driver will arrive so I can be home.
- As a customer, I am told when my order is next on the route so I can get ready.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** an order is out for delivery, **when** the customer opens tracking, **then** the driver's position is shown and is less than 30 seconds old.
- **Given** the driver is two stops away, **when** the route updates, **then** the arrival window narrows to 15 minutes.
- **Given** the customer's order is next, **when** the driver leaves the previous stop, **then** the customer gets a push notification within 1 minute.
- **Given** the order is delivered, **when** the driver marks it done, **then** their position is no longer shared with the customer.

## Out of scope

Live chat with the driver, and changing the delivery address once the order is out for delivery.
