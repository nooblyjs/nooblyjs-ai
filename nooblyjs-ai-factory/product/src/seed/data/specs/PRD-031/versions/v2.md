## Problem

Customers who shop after work find every same-day slot taken by mid-morning. September support tickets citing "no slots" reached 412.

## Goals

| Measure | Today | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same-day slots still open at 12:00 | 18% | 45% |
| Basket abandonment at slot step | 31% | 20% |

## User stories

- As a customer ordering after work, I can see which stores still have same-day capacity.
- As a customer, I can hold a slot while I finish checkout.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a slot is held, **when** the hold expires, **then** the slot returns to the pool.

## Out of scope

Dynamic slot pricing and courier partner integration.
